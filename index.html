Who We Are

Committed to community-led humanitarian action and sustainable development.

BONU Humanitarian and Community Development Organization is a community-focused organization based in Bule Hora, Oromia, Ethiopia. The organization seeks to contribute to the well-being of vulnerable communities through humanitarian assistance, community participation, youth empowerment, education, and sustainable development initiatives.

We believe that every individual deserves dignity, equal opportunity, and the chance to participate in building a better future. We aim to work with communities, local institutions, civil society organizations, and development partners to identify needs and promote practical solutions.

Our Mission, Vision and Goal

Our purpose and long-term commitment to communities.

Our Mission

To support vulnerable communities, promote human dignity, expand opportunities for children and young people, and strengthen local capacity through inclusive humanitarian and development initiatives.

Our Vision

A society in which all people can live with dignity, enjoy equal opportunities, and contribute to peaceful, resilient, and sustainable communities.

Our Goal

To contribute to improved community well-being through locally relevant programs, partnerships, participation, and accountable action.

Our Approach

We value community participation, collaboration, transparency, respect for human dignity, and solutions shaped by local priorities.

Our Areas of Work

Priority areas through which we seek to support communities.

Humanitarian Assistance

Supporting vulnerable people and communities affected by hardship through locally informed humanitarian initiatives.

Education and Child Support

Promoting educational opportunities and supporting children and students from vulnerable households.

Youth Empowerment

Encouraging skills development, leadership, participation, and access to livelihood opportunities for young people.

Women and Social Inclusion

Promoting inclusion, equal opportunity, dignity, and meaningful participation of women and marginalized groups.

Agriculture and Livelihoods

Supporting community resilience and exploring sustainable livelihood opportunities, including agriculture.

Environment and Resilience

Encouraging environmental awareness and community participation in protecting natural resources.
