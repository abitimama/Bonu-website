Who We Are

About BONU Organization

BONU Humanitarian and Community Development Organization is a community-focused civil society organization based in Ethiopia.

BONU seeks to work with communities, public institutions and development partners to support vulnerable people, create opportunities for young people and contribute to inclusive and sustainable development.

Our approach is guided by cooperation, dignity, participation, accountability and equal opportunity.

Responding to Community Priorities

Our thematic priorities connect humanitarian assistance with protection, peacebuilding and long-term community development.

Humanitarian Assistance

Supporting vulnerable households and communities through needs-based humanitarian assistance and emergency response.

Education Support

Promoting access to education and supporting students facing social and economic barriers.

Youth and Women Empowerment

Encouraging skills development, participation, leadership and livelihood opportunities.

Food Security and Livelihoods

Promoting resilient livelihoods, food security, agriculture and household wellbeing.

Environmental Protection

Supporting community-led natural resource protection and climate resilience initiatives.

Community Development

Working with communities to identify priorities and strengthen local participation and capacity.

Peacebuilding and Conflict Prevention

Our Geographic Reach

Working Beyond One Town

BONU aims to work with communities across Oromia and other parts of Ethiopia, according to community needs, organizational capacity, partnerships and applicable registration permissions.

Our Direction

Mission, Vision and Values

Our work is guided by dignity, fairness, accountability and participation.

Our Mission

To support vulnerable people, strengthen communities and promote inclusive development through humanitarian action, protection, peacebuilding, education and empowerment.

Our Vision

Communities across Ethiopia where people live with dignity, equal opportunities, peace, resilience and hope for a better future.

Our Values

Humanity, impartiality, neutrality, accountability, transparency, inclusion, respect and community participation.
