Who we are

Working alongside communities for lasting change.

BONU Humanitarian and Community Development Organization is an Ethiopia-focused civil society organization committed to supporting vulnerable communities and strengthening local capacity.

Our approach puts people at the centre: listening to community priorities, working with local structures, protecting dignity, and supporting solutions that can last.

We aim to work with communities, public institutions, civil society, and development partners across Ethiopia.

Connect with BONU
Our areas of work

Responding to urgent needs. Investing in stronger futures.

Our programme areas are designed around community priorities and will be delivered in partnership with relevant local stakeholders.

WASH

Improving access to safe water, sanitation, hygiene awareness, and community management of water services.

Food Security & Livelihoods

Supporting resilient livelihoods, household food security, climate-smart agriculture, and pastoralist communities.

Education & Child Development

Promoting access to inclusive learning, school support, child wellbeing, and opportunities for young people.

Peacebuilding & Conflict Prevention

Encouraging dialogue, local mediation, social cohesion, early response, and peaceful resolution of disputes.

General Protection

Promoting safety, dignity, inclusion, and referral pathways for children, women, older people, and at-risk groups.

Environment & Climate Resilience

Supporting ecosystem restoration, sustainable natural resource management, and community resilience to climate risks.

Women & Youth Empowerment

Building skills, leadership, participation, and pathways to safe and sustainable livelihoods.

Community Development

Working with communities to identify priorities and strengthen local services, participation, and resilience.

Humanitarian Assistance

Supporting coordinated, needs-based responses to emergencies, displacement, drought, and other shocks.

How we work

Trust is built through responsible action.

We aim to apply recognized humanitarian and development principles in all our work.

01

Listen first

Understand community priorities and include local voices in decisions.

02

Protect dignity

Promote safeguarding, do-no-harm practice, privacy, and equal access.

03

Work together

Coordinate with communities, government, civil society, and partners.

04

Be accountable

Monitor results, learn, communicate transparently, and improve.

Our commitment

Evidence, accountability and community voice.

We are committed to reporting honestly on our work and sharing verified results as programmes develop.

Transparency

Clear communication about activities, resources, partners, and results.

Safeguarding

Respectful conduct and safe ways for community members to raise concerns.

Learning

Monitoring and evaluation that helps us understand what works and what must improve.
