Who We Are

Working together for a stronger future

BONU Humanitarian and Community Development Organization is an Ethiopian civil society organization committed to humanitarian support and community development.

We believe communities should participate in identifying their priorities, shaping solutions and building a better future. Our work focuses on dignity, inclusion, accountability and locally led development.

  • Humanitarian assistance and community support
  • Education and opportunities for young people
  • Women’s empowerment and social inclusion
  • Climate resilience and environmental protection

Communities & Local Realities

Understanding different community needs

Ethiopia's communities have diverse livelihoods, cultures, environments and development priorities. BONU seeks to listen, learn and work alongside them.

Borana Communities

Pastoralist livelihoods, water scarcity, drought, pasture conditions, livestock health and food security.

Guji Communities

Livestock and farming livelihoods, youth opportunities, water access and sustainable natural resource management.

Gedeo Communities

Smallholder farming, coffee and agroforestry livelihoods, environmental protection and access to opportunities.

Burji Communities

Community-identified priorities, rural livelihoods, food security, education and inclusive development.
