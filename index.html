01 / ABOUT US

A future where every community can thrive.

BONU Humanitarian and Community Development Organization is a community-focused organization based in Bule Hora, Oromia, Ethiopia.

We seek to support vulnerable communities, encourage youth development and strengthen local capacity through inclusive and locally informed initiatives.
