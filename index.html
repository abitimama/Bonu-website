Who We Are

About BONU Organization

BONU Humanitarian and Community Development Organization is a community-focused initiative based in Bule Hora, Oromia, Ethiopia.

We are committed to supporting vulnerable communities, creating opportunities for young people and contributing to inclusive and sustainable development.

We believe that strong communities are built through cooperation, dignity, participation and equal opportunity.

Our Direction

Mission, Vision and Values

Our work is guided by dignity, fairness, accountability and participation.

Our Mission

To support vulnerable people, strengthen communities and promote inclusive development through humanitarian action, education and empowerment.

Our Vision

Communities where people live with dignity, equal opportunities, resilience and hope for a better future.

Our Goal

To contribute to sustainable improvements in education, livelihoods, youth participation and community wellbeing.
