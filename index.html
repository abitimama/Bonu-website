WHO WE ARE

Working Together for a Better Tomorrow

BONU Humanitarian and Community Development Organization is based in Bule Hora, Oromia, Ethiopia.

Our purpose is to support communities, promote human dignity and contribute to inclusive and sustainable development.

We seek to work with communities, public institutions, civil society organizations and development partners to identify local needs and develop practical responses.
