WHO WE ARE

About BONU

A community-focused organization committed to humanitarian support and sustainable development.

Our Organization

BONU Humanitarian and Community Development Organization is based in Bule Hora, Oromia, Ethiopia.

Our purpose is to support communities, promote human dignity and contribute to inclusive and sustainable development.

We seek to work with communities, public institutions, civil society organizations and development partners to identify local needs and develop practical responses.

OUR PURPOSE

Mission and Vision

Our Mission

To contribute to improved community wellbeing through humanitarian assistance, capacity development, inclusive participation and sustainable initiatives.

Our Vision

Communities where every person can live with dignity, access opportunities and participate in building a better future.

OUR PRIORITIES

Our Areas of Work

Our program priorities respond to community needs and will be developed with local participation and available resources.

Humanitarian Assistance

Supporting vulnerable households and communities affected by food insecurity and other humanitarian challenges.

Education and Youth

Promoting learning opportunities, youth participation and skills development.

Youth Development

Encouraging youth participation, practical skills and constructive engagement.

Livelihood Development

Encouraging local enterprise, agricultural initiatives and economic resilience.

Women and Children

Promoting inclusion, protection, equal opportunities and community support.

Environment

Supporting environmental awareness and sustainable community practices.

HOW WE WORK

Our Approach

We aim to build programs with communities and partners through responsible planning and learning.

Listen to communities
Understand local priorities and needs through consultation.
Plan together
Develop realistic activities with clear objectives and responsibilities.
Work with partners
Coordinate with relevant institutions and development organizations.
Monitor and learn
Review progress, document results and improve our work.
Promote accountability
Encourage transparency and responsible use of resources.
