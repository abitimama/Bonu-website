About BONU

BONU Humanitarian and Community Development Organization is committed to supporting vulnerable communities, promoting youth development and strengthening local capacity.

Our approach is based on dignity, inclusion, transparency and community participation.

Our Main Areas of Work

Humanitarian Assistance

Supporting vulnerable communities through needs-based assistance.

Youth Development

Promoting youth skills, participation and opportunities.

Community Development

Working with communities to strengthen local development.

Education Support

Promoting access to learning and educational opportunities.

Food Security

Supporting community resilience and food security initiatives.

Women and Social Inclusion

Encouraging equal participation and inclusive development.
